- ESPAltherma: evaporation and condensation in the details are the saturation temperatures at low and high pressure (`Low Pressure(T)`, `High Pressure(T)`), and no superheat or subcooling is derived from the pipe sensors any more. On a real EPRA12EAV3 the derived superheat read 0.1 K because the suction pipe sensor was below the evaporation temperature (#323, #254).
