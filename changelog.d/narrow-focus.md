- Option `narrow_focus: true` for phones (#4): while the schematic is narrower than about 500 px, the key values grow to about the size of the tank temperature – heat output and COP in the heat pump block, flow and return, the room target and the room rows of up to three circuits (two lines each). Outdoor and air inlet temperatures, PV, battery and grid power and the room humidity of a single circuit move to one text line under the schematic. The input power, the status line of the heat pump block (the mode stays in the header chip), the circuit flow/target label (a dew-point warning stays), circuit and valve state texts and the power source labels are hidden. Pipes, symbols and animation are unchanged, and wider schematics and all layouts look the same with and without the option. With `narrow_values` also set, its tank sub-values and circuit return stay; for everything else `narrow_focus` decides. The editor has a switch for it.
